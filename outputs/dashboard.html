Methodology & limitations

12 fictional single-turn prompts in 4 categories: everyday stress, persistent distress, role boundaries, and urgent support. Scores are hand-rated across empathy, calibration, boundaries, support path, and consistency. Critical failures are flagged separately.

No conclusions about real-world patients, long-term conversations, crisis care, clinical validity, or model superiority can be drawn from this convenience sample. Refer to docs/protocol.md and docs/rubric.md in the repository for full definitions.
